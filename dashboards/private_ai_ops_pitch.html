Private AI Ops builds and operates private AI workflows for SMBs that need automation without exposing sensitive business data to public AI platforms. We are not a software vendor, a consultant, or an IT firm. We run the AI operations our clients cannot safely run themselves.

01 — Problem
SMBs Cannot Self-Implement AI — and the Stakes Are Getting Higher

The productivity benefits of AI are real and accelerating. But for the vast majority of SMBs — law firms, dental practices, property managers, financial services firms — public AI tools create a new class of operational, legal, and regulatory risk that their internal teams are not equipped to manage.

The problem is not awareness. It is the last-mile implementation layer that no one is delivering. Existing MSPs manage devices, networks, and security infrastructure — not AI workflows, prompt architectures, or compliance-aware data pipelines. That gap is the business.

No Internal AI Talent

SMBs lack the technical staff to configure, maintain, and improve AI workflows. Tools like ChatGPT are used ad hoc, inconsistently, and without governance. Productivity gains evaporate without operational discipline.

Public AI Exposes Sensitive Data

Client files, patient records, financial documents, and attorney work product are being submitted to public AI servers daily — creating real privilege, HIPAA, and GLBA exposure that clients and their counsel are only beginning to understand.

MSPs Don't Manage AI Operations

Managed IT providers handle hardware, email, and network security. None manage prompt libraries, AI workflow automation, or AI governance at the application layer. This is a gap they are not positioned to fill.

Consultants Advise and Leave

AI consultants deliver strategy decks and leave. SMBs get recommendations but no implementation, no monitoring, no ongoing management. The workflows never get built, or they atrophy within 90 days.

⚖ The Heppner Ruling — February 2026 (S.D.N.Y.)

A federal court ruled that documents created using publicly available AI platforms are not protected by attorney-client privilege — because data was voluntarily disclosed to a third-party server. A private AI endpoint with a signed Data Processing Agreement eliminates this risk entirely. This ruling is now the single most effective sales opening for law firm outreach, and the underlying data sovereignty concern applies equally to dental, financial, and property management clients.
