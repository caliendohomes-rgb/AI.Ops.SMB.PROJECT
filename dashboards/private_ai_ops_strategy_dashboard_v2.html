Scenario overview
Low & Slow
Founder-only delivery. No vertical lead hires in Y1. One vertical at a time. Conservative 1–2 new clients/month. Infrastructure deployed only when signed. Prioritizes sustainable lifestyle business over aggressive growth.
All-In
Two vertical contractor leads hired in Mo 2. 4–6 new clients/month by Mo 4. Aggressive free-tool distribution. GPU infrastructure deployed Mo 3. Founder operating at ~50+ hrs/wk. Requires $30–50K working capital buffer.
